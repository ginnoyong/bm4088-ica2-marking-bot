## Scenario 1 — Delivery Performance

**Decision-maker:** Head of Operations
**Dataset:** dataset_01.zip

Your company is a mid-sized U.S. e-commerce platform that sells across multiple product categories. With growing order volumes, the Head of Operations has raised concerns about delivery performance — particularly the increasing number of delayed and cancelled orders affecting customer satisfaction scores and repeat purchase rates.

As analysts for your company, your team has been engaged by the Head of Operations to investigate delivery performance across the platform and develop data-driven recommendations to support operational decision-making. Your findings and recommendations should help the Head of Operations prioritise interventions that improve delivery reliability across the platform.

## Scenario 2 — Customer Basket Analysis

**Decision-maker:** Head of Marketing
**Dataset:** dataset_02.zip

Your company is a U.S. e-commerce platform serving a large and diverse customer base. The marketing team has observed that a significant proportion of customers purchase across multiple product categories in a single order, and that these multi-category orders tend to generate substantially higher order value. However, the team has limited understanding of the distinct buying behaviour patterns that exist across the customer base — and how these patterns could be leveraged to drive higher engagement and order value.

As analysts for your company, your team has been engaged by the Head of Marketing to analyse customer purchasing and basket behaviour across the platform and develop data-driven recommendations to support targeted marketing strategy. Your findings and recommendations should help the Head of Marketing identify distinct customer buying behaviour profiles and design campaigns that increase customer engagement and order value.

## Scenario 3 — Shipping Cost Optimisation

**Decision-maker:** Head of Logistics
**Dataset:** dataset_03.zip

Your company is a U.S. e-commerce platform managing a large network of sellers and delivering products across the country. The logistics team has flagged growing concerns about shipping costs — freight expenditure varies significantly across product categories and geographies, and for some categories, shipping costs are disproportionately high relative to product value, raising questions about the commercial viability of certain product-geography combinations.

As analysts for your company, your team has been engaged by the Head of Logistics to investigate shipping cost patterns across the platform and develop data-driven recommendations to support logistics and fulfilment decisions. Your findings and recommendations should help the Head of Logistics identify where to optimise shipping costs and improve the commercial efficiency of the platform's fulfilment operations.

## Scenario 4 — Product Portfolio Profitability

**Decision-maker:** Chief Financial Officer
**Dataset:** dataset_04.zip

Your company is a U.S. e-commerce platform offering products across seven categories. The finance team has raised concerns that the platform's revenue is heavily concentrated in a small number of categories — and that this concentration may be masking significant differences in profitability across the portfolio. A rebalancing of the product mix toward higher-margin categories could meaningfully improve overall platform profitability.

As analysts for your company, your team has been engaged by the Chief Financial Officer to analyse the financial performance of the product portfolio and develop data-driven recommendations to support portfolio investment decisions. Your findings and recommendations should help the CFO identify which product categories and characteristics are associated with stronger financial performance, and where to focus investment to improve overall platform profitability.

## Scenario 5 — Seasonal Performance

**Decision-maker:** Head of Operations Planning
**Dataset:** dataset_05.zip

Your company is a U.S. e-commerce platform that has experienced significant growth in order volumes over recent years. However, this growth has not been uniform — order volumes and cancellation rates show marked variation across seasons and years, creating operational challenges during peak periods that disproportionately affect platform performance and customer experience.

As analysts for your company, your team has been engaged by the Head of Operations Planning to investigate seasonal demand and performance patterns across the platform and develop data-driven recommendations to support operational planning. Your findings and recommendations should help the Head of Operations Planning anticipate peak period challenges and improve platform performance during high-demand seasons.

## Scenario 6 — Demand Patterns

**Decision-maker:** Head of Product Strategy
**Dataset:** dataset_06.zip

Your company is a U.S. e-commerce platform looking to refine its product strategy for the coming year. The product strategy team has limited visibility into how demand patterns differ across product categories and geographies — making it difficult to make informed decisions about inventory planning, category investment, and regional expansion.

As analysts for your company, your team has been engaged by the Head of Product Strategy to analyse how product characteristics and demand patterns vary across categories and regions and develop data-driven recommendations to support product portfolio decisions. Your findings and recommendations should help the Head of Product Strategy understand which product attributes and geographies drive demand, and where to focus investment to grow the portfolio strategically.
